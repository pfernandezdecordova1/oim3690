CR7 FAQ

Why is Ronaldo called CR7?

CR7 combines his initials (Cristiano Ronaldo) and his iconic shirt number 7.

Which clubs did he become most famous at?

Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and now Al Nassr are key parts of his career story.

What makes him special?

Elite work ethic, consistency, athleticism, and a huge number of goals at top level competitions.
